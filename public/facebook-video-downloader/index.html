Facebook downloader — frequently asked questions

Can I download private Facebook videos?

No. QuickSave can only save videos that are public. Videos shared to friends-only audiences or private groups cannot be fetched.

Do Facebook Reels work?

Yes — paste the Reel's share link and download it as an MP4, same as a regular video.

Why is there no HD option for my video?

HD appears only when the original upload was HD. If the uploader posted in SD, SD is the best quality that exists.

Can I save just the audio from a Facebook video?

Yes. Choose “Audio only (MP3)” after the formats load to extract the soundtrack.
