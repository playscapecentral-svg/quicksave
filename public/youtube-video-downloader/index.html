YouTube downloader, frequently asked questions

Can I download YouTube Shorts?

Yes. Paste the Shorts link into the box above exactly like a normal video link, then choose MP4 or MP3.

Why do some downloaders give silent 1080p files?

YouTube stores high resolutions as video-only streams. QuickSave automatically merges the best audio with your chosen video quality, so the MP4 you save always has sound.

What is the highest quality I can download?

Whatever the video itself offers, up to 4K (2160p) plus HDR sources where available. QuickSave lists every format the video provides.

Can I download a whole playlist?

QuickSave downloads one video at a time. Paste each video’s own link; playlists themselves are not fetched.

Do age-restricted or private videos work?

No. Only public videos can be downloaded. Private, unlisted-without-access, age-restricted or region-locked videos may fail: YouTube blocks them itself.
